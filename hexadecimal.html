Lesson Notes

Red Task: Hexadecimal Basics & 4-Bit

Answer the basic facts and convert the 4-bit binary nibbles into a single Hexadecimal digit (0-9, A-F).

Amber Task: 8-Bit Binary to Hexadecimal

Convert the 8-bit binary numbers into 2-digit Hexadecimal codes.
(Tip: Split the 8 bits down the middle into two 4-bit nibbles!)

Green Task: Hexadecimal to Binary

Convert these 2-digit Hexadecimal codes into 8-bit binary.
(Tip: Convert each hex letter/number into 4 bits and push them together)

Blue Task: Denary Conversions & Theory

Challenge yourself! You will need to convert between Denary and Hexadecimal directly.
