Amber Task: Computational Thinking in Everyday Life

You use computational thinking outside Computing lessons too. Read each situation and explain what you would do. Include the key ideas in your answer.
Example: Getting ready for school

Decomposition: Get dressed, eat breakfast and pack my bag.

Pattern recognition: I need the same equipment on the same school days.

Abstraction: I only need to check the things required for that day.

Algorithm: Follow my morning routine in the correct order.

Green Task: Plan a School Disco

Your school is organising a disco. Use the four computational thinking techniques to help plan it. Write in full sentences where you can.
The problem: There are lots of things to organise for a school disco. You need to decide what matters, split the work into smaller jobs, spot useful patterns and put instructions in the right order.
