Green Task: Design a Better Lego Building Process

A student is building a complex Lego model containing 800 pieces. They keep losing pieces, repeat steps by mistake and struggle to understand the instructions.

Apply your knowledge of computational thinking to improve the process. Some questions require you to identify a pillar and explain your reasoning.

Challenge: In your own words, explain how using decomposition, pattern recognition and abstraction together could make building the Lego model easier.

Blue Task: Design a Homework Planner App

Scenario: Your school has asked you to design a homework planner app. Students should be able to record homework, set deadlines, receive reminders and see which tasks they need to complete first. Some students forget to enter deadlines, while others have several assignments due on the same day.

Your task is to use computational thinking to plan a solution. Give specific details and explain your decisions.

Success criteria: Your answers should use accurate technical vocabulary, apply the pillars to the scenario, explain your decisions and demonstrate logical reasoning.
