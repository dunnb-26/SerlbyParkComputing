Step 1: Open the Workspace

Go to your Microsoft Teams class and open the assignment for today.
Open the attached PPT Template to complete the tasks below, then paste your screenshots here!

Red Task ✅ Teacher Verified

Add 3 new slides to your presentation using the 'Title and Content' layout.

On slide 2, add the title "My Hobbies" and create a bulleted list of 3 things you like to do.

Paste a screenshot showing your completed Slide 2:

Click this box to select it, then press Ctrl+V to paste screenshot

Amber Task ✅ Teacher Verified

Go to the Transitions tab at the top of the screen.

Select a transition you like (e.g., Fade or Push) and click "Apply To All" so every slide uses it.

Paste a screenshot of your Transitions ribbon showing it has been applied:

Click this box to select it, then press Ctrl+V to paste screenshot

Green Task ✅ Teacher Verified

Insert an image onto Slide 3. Go to the Animations tab and apply an 'Entrance' animation to the picture.

Click the Animation Pane button so the side menu appears.

Paste a screenshot showing your image and the Animation Pane open:

Click this box to select it, then press Ctrl+V to paste screenshot

Blue Task ✅ Teacher Verified

Go to the View tab and click on Slide Master.

Change the background color of the top master slide, and change the Title font to a style of your choice. Close the Master View when finished.

Paste a screenshot of your screen while inside the Slide Master view:

Click this box to select it, then press Ctrl+V to paste screenshot
