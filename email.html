Step 1: Open Outlook

Open Microsoft Outlook or your school email web app to complete the tasks below, then paste your screenshots into the boxes!

Red Task ✅ Teacher Verified

Click on 'New Email'.

Type my email address in the "To" box (dunnb@serlbyparkacademy.org.uk), and type "Test Email" in the Subject line.

Paste a screenshot showing your new email with the recipient and subject filled out:

Click this box to select it, then press Ctrl+V to paste screenshot

Amber Task ✅ Teacher Verified

Add in the subject 'Test Email'.

Write a short sentence in the body explaining what an email is and what you might use one for.

Paste a screenshot showing your email body definition sentence:

Click this box to select it, then press Ctrl+V to paste screenshot

Green Task ✅ Teacher Verified

Create a signature under 'Insert' then 'Signature' with your full name and form group.

Insert it into your email.

Paste a screenshot showing your email signature inserted:

Click this box to select it, then press Ctrl+V to paste screenshot

Blue Task ✅ Teacher Verified

Download an image from the internet (right click, Save image as, save to Downloads).

Go to 'Insert' and under attachments, find and attach the downloaded image.

Paste a screenshot showing your attached image:

Click this box to select it, then press Ctrl+V to paste screenshot
